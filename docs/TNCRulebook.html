TNC Rulebook

Tennis Nerds is built around respectful competition, positive energy, inclusivity, and genuine community spirit. Whether you join our tournaments, socials, WhatsApp groups, or online discussions, these guidelines help create an environment where everyone enjoys the game.

🎾 Respect Everyone

Treat all members, organisers, opponents, and venues respectfully. Healthy competition is encouraged, but personal attacks and toxic behaviour are not.

  • No harassment, bullying, or intimidation
  • No discrimination or hate speech
  • Respect different skill levels and backgrounds

🏆 Play Fair

Tennis Nerds promotes fair competition and good sportsmanship both on and off court.

  • Follow tournament and match rules
  • Report scores honestly
  • Communicate clearly with opponents
  • Avoid intentionally manipulating results

🤝 Inclusive Community

Players of all abilities and backgrounds are welcome in Tennis Nerds.

  • Support beginners respectfully
  • No gatekeeping or mocking others
  • Help create a welcoming atmosphere

📱 Social & Online Conduct

Our online groups should remain friendly, useful, and tennis-focused.

  • No spam or excessive self-promotion
  • No abusive or inflammatory posts
  • Respect members’ privacy
  • Keep discussions constructive

📍 Event & Venue Behaviour

Please respect public facilities, venues, organisers, and court bookings.

  • Arrive on time where possible
  • Leave courts clean and tidy
  • Respect venue staff and rules
  • Communicate cancellations responsibly

🛡 Safety & Privacy

Member safety is important both online and during events.

  • Respect personal boundaries
  • Do not share private information without consent
  • Report unsafe or inappropriate behaviour

Built Around Community

Tennis Nerds exists to help people play more tennis, improve their game, meet new people, and enjoy the sport in a positive environment. Every member contributes to the atmosphere we create together.

Questions or Concerns?

If you experience behaviour that violates these guidelines, please contact the Tennis Nerds organisers directly.
