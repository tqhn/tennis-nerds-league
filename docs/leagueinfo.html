Welcome to the Tennis Nerds League

County-wide Box Leagues Network

Our box league format delivers County-wide competitive depth with the absolute flexibility of local, self-arranged scheduling. We maintain a high-standard, high-intensity environment where players can actively improve their ITF WTN rating, test their game against regional opponents, and integrate into a thriving tennis community.

TNL: Hertfordshire TNL: North London

Open Singles Box League

For all players over 18.

Players will be grouped into boxes (typically 5-7 players per group), and each player plays every other player in their box during the round.

!! IMPORTANT: League of Areté - This league is exclusively for player members committed to achieving Excellence in Action and can ensure all matches are arranged and completed within the round deadline.

How It Works

  • 1. Joining the league: New players can join at the start of any round or during the round if there is availability in a box.
  • 2. Dynamic Box Grouping: There is no traditional promotion or relegation based on box standings. Instead, players are dynamically re-grouped entirely by their current ITF WTN rating at the start of every single round. Your box assignment naturally reflects your current form.
  • 3. Match Scheduling: Upon acceptance, you will be added strictly to your specific league's WhatsApp network (TNL: Herts or TNL: North London) to coordinate fixtures directly with your box opponents.
  • 4. Court Booking & Fees: Public court booking fees are split strictly 50/50 between both players. If a player chooses to host a match at their private home club, a split should mutually be agreed upon in advance.
  • 5. Travel & Venue Selection: To reward proactivity, the player who initiates contact first to schedule the match has priority choice of venue & time.
  • 6. The 48-Hour Communication Rule: To honor the League of Areté, players must acknowledge match invitations within 48 hours. If a player completely "ghosts" communication for more than 7 days, the active player can claim a walkover win.
  • 7. Late Cancellations: Any cancellation made with less than 24 hours' notice results in a forfeit (3 points to the non-canceling player, 0 to the canceling player). The player who canceled is responsible for the full cost of the lost court booking.
  • 8. Match Format: Best of 3 standard sets. Because court bookings are strictly timed (typically 1 or 2 hours), players may mutually agree to play a 10-point Match Tiebreak in place of a full 3rd set if they risk running out of court time.
  • 9. League Points: Win (or contested walkover/retirement) = 3 points, Loss (or contested walkover/retirement) = 1 point, Uncontested Walkover = 3 points (awarded if an opponent is entirely unresponsive for 7+ days or cancels late)
  • 10. On-Court Rules & Self-Umpiring (LTA/ITF Code of Conduct): Matches are completely self-umpired. In the spirit of Areté, players must conduct themselves with absolute integrity using the following protocols.
  • 11. Line Calls: You are solely responsible for calling lines on your side of the net. If in doubt, the ball is IN. You must never ask your opponent to make a call on your side, and you cannot claim a "let" or replay a point because a call was difficult.
  • 12. Foot Faults: Because matches are self-umpired, a player cannot officially call a foot fault on their opponent. However, constant stepping over the baseline during a serve violates the spirit of fair play. If an opponent is explicitly foot-faulting, mention it politely between games; do not stop play or deduct points mid-game.
  • 13. Hindrance: An intentional or loud distraction while a player is hitting a stroke (e.g., shouting out during a rally, talking mid-point, or dropping a racquet) results in the immediate loss of the point for the player causing the hindrance. Unintentional hindrances (like a ball rolling onto the court from an adjacent court) result in an immediate "Let" and the point is replayed..
  • 14. Reporting Results: Winners must enter the final score into the LTA Portal within 24 hours of match completion. In the event of a scoring dispute during a match, players should follow LTA self-officiating principles (e.g., if in doubt, the ball is 'in'). For administrative disputes, the decision of the Tennis Nerds committee is final.
  • 15. Unfinished Matches: If court time expires mid-match, the player leading when play stops claims the win. To log this on the LTA Portal, enter the exact score achieved, and the winner will input the remaining games as a "Retirement" victory (Note: At least one full set must be completed for the match to count toward your official WTN).
