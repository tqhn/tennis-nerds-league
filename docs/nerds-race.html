Race Points

How Points Are Allocated

  • Tournament Champion: 100 Points
  • Tournament Runner-Up: 80 Points
  • Semi-Finalists: 60 Points
  • Quarter-Finalists: 40 Points
  • Round of 16 / Group Stage Wins: 20 Points
  • Tournament Appearance / Round of 32+: 10 Points

💡 LTA Grade Multipliers: Standard points apply to Grade 6 open events (1.0x). Points for Grade 7 or box leagues are calculated at 0.5x.

The Nerds Race Champion

There is no separate finals event. The season ends with the Autumn Showdown, and once it is complete the player leading each regional table is crowned Nerds Race Champion for that region.

Every match you play across the season counts towards your total, so the title is decided purely by the Nerds Race leaderboard, rewarding year-long commitment and consistency.

🛡 The Tie-Breaker Rule: If players sit tied on total points at the conclusion of the Autumn Showdown, the individual with the highest aggregate match-win percentage throughout the year is ranked higher, and takes the Nerds Race Champion title if the tie is at the top.
