Your Journey to the All England Club

"Play Your Way to Wimbledon is the UK's largest individual mass-participation tournament. Organized by Tennisnerds as an open competition, players compete in venue stages across Hertfordshire and North London for a chance to advance through Area & County Finals and winners of which all the way to the National Finals on the famous grass courts of Wimbledon."

Event Information & Guidance

Key Rules & Match Format

  • Events: Adult Men's Doubles & Women's Doubles (Open Entry).
  • Scoring & Match Format: Best of two tiebreak sets (first to 6 games, tiebreak at 6-6), with a 10-point match tiebreak played in place of a third set.
  • Cost: Free to enter via the official LTA Competition Management System (CMS).
  • Eligibility: Open to adult players (LTA Advantage registered). Designed as an inclusive, open-access pathway. All players must have an ITF World Tennis Number between 25.0 – 40.9 at the closing deadline.
  • Venues: Staged across club/park venues in Hertfordshire and North London (Final venue details released post-registration).
  • Official Competition Rules: For full LTA eligibility guidelines, regulations, and FAQs, visit the Official LTA Play Your Way to Wimbledon Page.

Awards & Prizes

  • Venue Stage Champions & Runners-Up: Official LTA Play Your Way to Wimbledon winners' certificates/prizes and Tennisnerds digital honours.
  • Area & County Qualification: Venue stage champions advance directly to represent our venue at the Area/County Finals in June/July.
  • The Ultimate Prize (SW19): Regional/County champions qualify for the National Finals (2–8 August) to compete on the world-famous grass courts at The All England Lawn Tennis Club, Wimbledon—complete with official Wimbledon player hospitality and experiences.

How to Enter

  1. Sign up your doubles pair through the official LTA CMS link above.
  2. Compete in local round-robin or knockout venue stage matches between March and May.
  3. Venue stage winners qualify directly for the Area/County Finals in June.
🛡️ Official LTA Fair Play & Rules: All matches follow LTA Grade 6 tournament guidelines. Entries must be submitted through the official LTA CMS before the publishing and registration deadline. Respect court booking procedures and uphold fair play at all times.
